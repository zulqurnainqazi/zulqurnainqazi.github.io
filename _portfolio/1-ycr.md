I founded the **[Youth Center for Research (YCR)](https://youthcfr.com)** in 2019 as a global platform for youth empowerment through research, advocacy, and communication.

- Designed and have run the flagship **Annual Research Program**, a virtual exchange connecting youth and experts, every year since 2019 — with nearly 100 alumni from around 27 countries.
- Built collaborations with universities and organizations in Zambia, Nigeria, Pakistan, and Indonesia.
