As part of the World Bank's technical assistance to the Reform Support Unit, Government of Sindh (2014–2018), I:

- Led the design of an internal communication system to improve data sharing among 138 districts and 11 departments.
- Monitored and evaluated 120 deliverables across 8 sub-departments; recommendations approved by the Education Secretary contributed to a 30% improvement in program execution.
- Contributed to a baseline study of the provincial education communication infrastructure and the Reforms Implementation Plan for the School Education & Literacy Department (2016).
