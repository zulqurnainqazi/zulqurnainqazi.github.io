I have taught and designed undergraduate courses in public policy, education planning, social theory, and South Asian studies in Pakistan, and taught at The Ohio State University. I design learning around inquiry — helping students ask good questions, work with evidence, and connect ideas to public life. Through the Youth Center for Research, I also mentor young people conducting their first research projects.
