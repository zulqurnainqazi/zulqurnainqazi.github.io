I am a **Fulbright-funded Ph.D. candidate in Education Policy at The Ohio State University** (expected June 2027). My research focuses on **public policy and policy analysis**, **policy advocacy and communication**, and the **internationalization of higher education** — in particular, how international students navigate immigration policy, employability, and the transition from study to work.

My dissertation is a comparative case study of international master's students in STEM-designated programs in the United States. Drawing on an aspirations, capabilities, and agency framework, it examines how students build employability and navigate the U.S. job market under time pressure and policy uncertainty.

Before my doctoral studies, I taught public policy, education planning, and social theory at **Habib University** in Karachi, where I remain an Affiliated Lecturer, and worked on World Bank–supported education reform with the Government of Sindh. I am the Founding Director of the **[Youth Center for Research](https://youthcfr.com)**, a global platform that builds transnational learning communities for young researchers, and a Policy Intern at the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce.

I hold a Master of Public Administration and Public Policy from the University of York as a **Chevening Scholar**, and a certificate in Leadership, Policy and Communication from the University of Cambridge through the **Future Leaders Connect Fellowship**.

Research interests
------
- Internationalization of higher education and international student mobility
- Employability preparedness and post-study transitions
- Agent–structure interactions and immigration policy
- Education policy analysis, including ICT and digital tools in education
- Youth empowerment and transnational research collaboration
